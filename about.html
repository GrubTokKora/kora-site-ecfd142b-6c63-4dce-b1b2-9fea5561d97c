About Prime Power Devices

Founded in 2001, Prime Power Devices manufactures power transistors in TO-3, TO-3P, and TO-220 packages. Over the past two decades, we have built our business around consistent quality, reliable products, and a strong understanding of power semiconductor manufacturing.

For more than 24 years, we have worked as a trusted subcontractor to Bharat Electronics Ltd. (BEL), supplying devices for applications in the defense and communication sectors. This experience has given us a deep understanding of the quality and reliability required for critical applications.

Our products are used by OEMs across telecommunications, broadcasting, public address systems, industrial electronics, and defense.

Today, Prime Power Devices is building on this experience to market power devices under our own brand. We remain focused on what has always been important to us: good products, consistent quality, dependable performance, and responsive customer support.

About Narotham Reddy

With over 40 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Narotham Reddy has built a career rooted in innovation, reliability, and quality. He began his journey in 1980 with SSDIL (later Greaves Semiconductor) and went on to work with ETTDC, Keltron, and Teamasia Lakhi Semiconductor. In 2001, he founded Prime Power Devices, focusing on high-quality TO-3 packaged power transistors for Bharat Electronics Ltd. and other leading clients. Under his leadership, the company has grown into TO-3P and TO-220 device manufacturing, expanding its reach in both Indian and global markets. An Electronics and Communications Engineering graduate from Osmania University, Hyderabad, Narotham Reddy continues to lead Prime Power Devices with a commitment to excellence and long-term partnerships.
